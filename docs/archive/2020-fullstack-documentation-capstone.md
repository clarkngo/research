## Notes

This is the extended capstone report underlying the peer-reviewed
paper [Decreasing the Barrier to Entry for an Open-Source Full-Stack Web
Development](/publications/2021-open-source-fullstack-documentation)
(ISCAP 2021, Vol. 14, Paper 5540) — kept here for the full methodology,
diagrams, and raw survey results that didn't fit in the condensed
proceedings version. Exact completion date is approximate; not printed
in the document itself.
