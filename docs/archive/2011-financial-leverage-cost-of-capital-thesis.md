  Bachelor's thesis (BS Management of Financial Institutions) examining
  the impact of financial leverage on cost of capital, price-earnings
  ratio, and market value for firms under the Construction,
  Infrastructure & Allied Services sub-sector of the Philippine Stock
  Exchange (EEI Corporation, Holcim Philippines, Phinma Corporation,
  Republic Cement Corporation, Southeast Asia Cement Holdings, TKC Steel
  Corporation, and Vulcan Industrial & Mining) for the period 2001–2009,
  and determines each firm's optimal capital structure using the cost
  of capital approach.
