  can reduce the "chasm" between FinTech and finance professionals,
  building on Moore's technology adoption lifecycle. The team develops a
  FinTech demo system using these technologies and analyzes it using
  Grounded Theory and Evidence-Based Practice, proposing a target system
  — secure, scalable, maintainable, and trustworthy — that meets the
  practical expectations of finance professionals rather than the
  visionary expectations of FinTech's early adopters.
