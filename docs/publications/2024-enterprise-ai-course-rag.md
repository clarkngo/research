## Notes

Presented as a poster at the US-Korea Conference (UKC) 2024 on Science,
Technology, and Entrepreneurship, hosted by KSEA, in San Francisco, CA, August 21–24, 2024. Full proceedings volume:
[UKC2024_Proceeding.pdf](https://ukc2024.ksea.org/programs/proceeding-ukc2024).

## Course materials

The course design was delivered in two formats:

- Class version (DIT637, ten modules): [github.com/clarkngo/cs-courses/tree/main/dit637](https://github.com/clarkngo/cs-courses/tree/main/dit637)
- Workshop version (RAG + MLOps hands-on lab): [github.com/clarkngo/rag-mlops](https://github.com/clarkngo/rag-mlops)

## Self-paced courses

The author later built free, self-paced courses that follow the same
design as the university versions:

- [Smart & Secure Systems: One App from Cloud to RAG](https://clarkngo.github.io/courses/smart-secure-systems/):
  10 modules, 20 lessons, paralleling the DIT637 class. A single
  movie-search app grows from a React page through mobile, full-stack,
  DevSecOps CI/CD, cloud deployment, ML recommendations, MLOps, a
  self-hosted LLM, and RAG.
- [AI Engineer Quick Start](https://clarkngo.github.io/courses/ai-engineer-quick-start/):
  5 modules, 10 lessons, paralleling the RAG + MLOps workshop. It covers
  AI/ML foundations, how LLMs work, RAG, and MLOps, then builds a
  movie-recommender chatbot with Ollama, ChromaDB, MLflow, and FastAPI.
