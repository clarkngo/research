abstract: >
  A research and development plan for an interactive course platform
  that uses AI to help students connect the mathematics of data science
  (linear algebra, analytic geometry, matrix decompositions, vector
  calculus, probability, continuous optimization, and probabilistic
  modeling) to applied machine learning. The plan identifies nine
  friction points students hit when moving from theory to ML, maps a
  10-module curriculum to code labs and interactive visualizations, and
  specifies four AI features: a Socratic "muddiest point" tutor, a
  gradient-descent simulator with an AI coach, a matrix-decomposition
  visualizer with narrated geometry, and an in-browser notebook with a
  graduated hint ladder. Every math claim the AI makes is checked with
  a computer algebra system or recomputed in the browser. It also
  proposes an A/B study design with four research questions and
  includes a working gradient-descent prototype.
